Result Workflow

Teacher → HOD Review → HOD Approves → HOD Sends → Examination Officer → Finalize → Administrator → Publish
CA1 (15) + CA2 (15) + Examination (70) = 100 marks.

Teachers enter results and submit them to the appropriate HOD.

The HOD reviews and approves the result. Approval does NOT automatically send the result to the Examination Officer.

After approving, the HOD must manually click Send to Examination Officer .
